How pricing works

  • You pay per result, not per compute minute. The price in the table is the whole story, plus the per-run start fee.
  • Every row carries a charged flag. Rows that don't clear the actor's check (wrong company, incomplete job, duplicate) are still delivered so you can see them, at $0.
  • Every actor reads public pages only. There is no login to hand over, so no account of yours is at risk.
